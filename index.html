    Quick tips

    • Banks and delivery services never ask for passwords or OTP codes by text.
    • Shortened links (bit.ly, tinyurl) hide the real destination — be cautious.
    • Urgency ("act now", "account suspended") is a classic pressure tactic.
    • When in doubt, contact the company directly using a number from their official site, not the message.
